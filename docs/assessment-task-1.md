## Acceptance

User-spec precedence clarification: explicitly owned assumptions are permissible inputs for calculation, not a substitute for accepted supporting evidence when declaring investment readiness. Material inputs without accepted evidence block `Recommend investment`, including when provenance is `assumed` with an owner. Preserve the assumptions and show their uncertainty; require evidence or validation before investment designation. Add explicit opportunity risk readiness (`unknown` / `ready` / `concern`, default `unknown`), separate from criticalControlsOpen: absence of a recorded critical finding is not proof that risk was assessed. The prior policy wording permitting owned assumptions alone is superseded by this clarification.

Clarification against the approved explicit-cost requirement: when review minutes exceed the gross handling-time reduction, the clamp in the formula above must not hide extra work. Return actionable incomplete requiring additional effort to be costed, or explicitly model its negative capacity impact. Add a regression case.
